# 🚀 ResumeLens AI

ResumeLens AI is an AI-powered resume analysis and interview preparation platform built using the MERN Stack and Google Gemini AI. It helps candidates prepare smarter by analyzing their resume against a target job description and generating personalized interview preparation.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students prepare for interviews by searching random interview questions online. Most of these questions are general and do not match the specific job role they are applying for. Because of this, students spend time preparing topics that may not be useful for their interviews.

ResumeLens AI solves this problem by analyzing a candidate's resume and the target job description using Google Gemini AI. It generates role-specific technical and behavioral interview questions, calculates a resume match score, identifies missing skills, and creates a personalized learning roadmap. This helps candidates focus on the right topics and prepare more effectively for their interviews.
